Our Story

Built on trust, driven by community

Friends and Family Handymen was born from a simple idea: treat every customer like a neighbor. Because that's exactly what they are.

We started out doing small jobs for friends and family around town. Word spread quickly — not because we advertised, but because people noticed the difference. We show up when we say we will. We quote fairly. And we don't leave until the job is done right.

Today we serve homes throughout the area with the same values we started with. Fair prices, prompt service, and work you can be proud of.
