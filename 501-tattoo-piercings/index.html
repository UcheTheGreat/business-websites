About the Shop

501 Tattoo & Piercings is more than a tattoo shop — it's a creative space where you can feel comfortable expressing yourself.

Our studio is spotlessly clean, our artists are experienced and passionate, and every client is treated like family. We use only premium inks and single-use needles for your safety.

Whether it's your first tattoo or your fiftieth, we'll make sure the experience is as incredible as the art we create.
